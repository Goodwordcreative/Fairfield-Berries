Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about picking, prices, ice cream and visiting Fairfield Berries.

How much does the ice cream cost?

Adult ice creams are $6.50 and kiddie cones are $4. A chocolate flake is $1 extra. Add-ons and speciality flavours incur additional costs. You can also check the Prices page or contact the farm.

What real fruit ice cream flavours do you have?

Our flavours include mixed berry, raspberry, strawberry, boysenberry, banana, feijoa, coffee, mocha, honey and choc berry. See the ice cream section and check Instagram for the latest updates.

What berries can we pick right now?

Our approximate Pick Your Own season runs from December to February during opening hours, with strawberries, raspberries and blackberries subject to seasonal availability. Check current availability before visiting. See Pick Your Own and the latest Instagram updates.

Are your berries spray-free?

Yes, Fairfield Berries has confirmed its berries are spray-free. For questions about growing practices, please contact the farm.

What are your opening hours?

Until 30 November, we are open Saturdays and Sundays, 11am–4pm. From 1 December, we are open every day, 9am–5.30pm. Check Instagram for current opening hours before visiting.

Where is Fairfield Berries?

292 Wakefield-Kohatu Highway, Wai-iti 7095, on the main highway about 3 minutes south of Wakefield or 20 minutes south of Richmond. View map and directions

Do you sell frozen berries?

Yes. The farm shop offers fresh and frozen berries. Explore berries

How much does Pick Your Own cost?

Boysenberries are $20 per family punnet and raspberries $22 per family punnet. There is no entry fee. See the Prices page

Can I bring my own container?

No. Please purchase a punnet from the shop before picking. One punnet is required for every two people. Picking rules.

Do you have other ice cream options?

We offer a yoghurt option, cups or cones, kiddie cones, dairy-free vegan gelato and takeaway tubs. See ice cream options.

Are your gifts and shop products local?

Yes! Our gifts and local stockist products come from makers and producers in the Nelson Tasman region. Thank you for supporting local. Explore our local products.

How can I get in touch?

Call 03-541 8564, email fairfieldberries@gmail.com, or use the contact page.
