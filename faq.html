Frequently Asked Questions

Find out more about our fruit, local stalls, wholesale orders and facilities.

Are your berries spray-free?

We are not spray-free. We follow a standard spray programme for our crops, including fungicides and insecticides, as well as targeted weed spraying where needed. We strictly adhere to withholding periods for chemicals, and our fruit is residue tested to ensure it is within tolerances. While we would love to be spray-free, it wouldn't be commercially viable to do so.

Do you sell fresh second-grade fruit (for jam)?

Yes, we do. You'll find this for sale in our shop.

Do you sell frozen fruit?

Yes, we do. All are packed in 1 kg bags and range from $10–$16.

Do you sell jam?

Yes, we sell our very own homemade jam using our own berries.

Do you sell berries wholesale?

Yes, we do. Please send us an email enquiry at fairfieldberries@gmail.com.

Yes, we do, usually in 8 kg or 10 kg quantities. Please text Elise on 027 636 7214 to arrange.

Do you have pre-picked and packed berries if I don't want to pick my own?

Yes, for sure. You'll find these for sale in the chiller in our shop.
